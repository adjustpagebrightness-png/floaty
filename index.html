How to use

  1. Install Floaty and open any YouTube video.
  2. Click the Floaty button in the player's control bar, or press Alt + P.
  3. Move and resize the floating window anywhere. Close it and the video goes back into the page.

Private by design

Floaty doesn't collect, store or send your personal data. No account, no tracking, no ads. It only runs on youtube.com. Read the privacy policy.
